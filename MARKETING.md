# Zazu launch marketing kit

Graphics are in `scripts/marketing/out/` (rebuild with `python scripts/marketing/make_marketing.py`). Everything below is written for the **public launch**, when Zazu is live on Google Play. Swap `[Play link]` for the real store URL. Until then, don't post anything that says "on Google Play" or "free to download".

Facts the copy relies on, so it stays true: free, Android only, nearly 400 words in the free library, no ads, no tracking, a gentle chime, snooze, Word Gym. Zazu Gold is **coming soon** (ten packs, 1,741 words). Never give a Gold price until it's on sale.

## 3. Paid ad drafts

Use these after launch, once the closed test is over and the listing is public. Start small (£5 a day) and judge on cost per install and day-7 retention, not clicks.

### Google Ads, App campaign (Google Play)

Google mixes these assets automatically. Limits: headlines 30 characters, descriptions 90.

Headlines:
```
Wake up to a new word
An alarm that teaches you
One word every morning
Learn a word before breakfast
Free word-of-the-day alarm
```

Descriptions:
```
Your alarm goes off, you meet a new word, answer one question, and the day begins.
Nearly 400 words with meanings and roots. Free, with no ads and no tracking.
A gentle alarm clock for people who love words. Learn one properly every morning.
Words like petrichor, laconic and mellifluous, one each morning. Free on Android.
```

Images: `hero-square.png`, `hero-landscape.png`, and two or three word cards (`word-*-feed.png`). Add the Play screenshots too.

Creative: `word-*-story.png` and `hero-story.png` for Stories and Reels placements, and `word-*-feed.png` and `hero-square.png` for feed. Test a word card against the phone graphic. Word cards usually feel less like ads.

Targeting to start: UK, 18 to 45, interests in reading, writing, crosswords, Wordle, language learning, etymology. Android only.

## 4. Rules for anything new

- UK English, no em dashes, no hype words (see `writing-rules.md`).
- Only claim what's in the free app today. Gold is "coming soon" until it's on sale.
- Use real words and real etymology from `zazu-words.json`. Don't make one up for an ad.
- Screenshots come from a real phone, not the web build.
